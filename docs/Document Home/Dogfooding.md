# Rpi Dogfooding Guide

## Introduction

Welcome to the Rpi Dogfooding Process Guide! This document provides a comprehensive overview of the internal testing phase for the Rpi project. Our primary objective is to leverage the insights and feedback from our internal users to identify potential issues and enhance the product's quality before its public release. By engaging in this dogfooding process, we aim to deliver a robust and user-friendly product that meets the highest standards of excellence.

Throughout this document, you will find detailed guidelines on how to participate in the dogfooding process, including instructions on reporting issues, providing feedback, and accessing support resources. We encourage all team members to actively engage in this process and contribute to the continuous improvement of the Rpi project.
